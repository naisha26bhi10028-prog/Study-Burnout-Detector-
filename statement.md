# StudyWell — Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students may focus strongly on academic performance while overlooking study duration, sleep, stress, breaks, and screen time. StudyWell provides a simple interactive platform for reviewing these selected patterns together and identifying areas that may require attention.

## Scope
The system includes assessment input, rule-based scoring, risk classification, risk-factor identification, recommendations, CSV storage, assessment history, and trend visualisation.

It does not include medical or psychological diagnosis, professional medical advice, real-time monitoring, automatic background tracking, AI-based diagnosis, or cloud user accounts.

## Target Users
Students who want an educational self-assessment of selected study and routine patterns.

## High-Level Features
- Student assessment
- Rule-based risk analysis
- Risk-factor identification
- Recommendations
- Assessment history
- Trend visualisation
- Modular Streamlit dashboard
